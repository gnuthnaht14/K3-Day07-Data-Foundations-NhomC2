Fees and Charges

The Student Fees and Charges Guide (PDF, 803KB, 65p) explains how fees are set and what the various charges are for the 2025 academic year. The Guide includes information about non-academic fees (such as international insurance fees and Vietnam insurance fees) and other administrative fees (such as transcript fees, graduation fees, and photocopying fees).

A list of the indicative total program fees, calculated at today's course costs, can be viewed under Tuition Fees. Students can view their fees, download invoices and make online payments via the Financial Account tile in myRMIT.

Note: RMIT reserves the right to adjust program tuition fees to take into account increases in University and program delivery costs. All fee increases are subject to approval by the RMIT Board of Directors.
