Change your enrolment

After you have enrolled, you can vary your enrolment until the relevant cut-off dates for adding or dropping courses each semester. Learn more on how to drop a course (PDF, 623KB).

Important notifications and class schedules will be sent to you via email after your enrolment/re-enrolment is completed. You can also check the important dates via myRMIT.

How to change your enrolment: 

Visit the Enrolment tile in myRMIT

If you enrolled via the paper-based process or cannot make changes via myRMIT, you can vary your enrolment by filling out the Enrolment Variation form (PDF, 233KB), and then submitting it to Student Connect.

If you need further support, please contact Student Connect.

Withdraw your program enrolment (student-initiated)

If you wish to withdraw completely from your studies, you must cancel your program enrolment. If you’re considering cancelling your program enrolment because of stress or personal issues, please be aware that RMIT has a range of free support services for students that might be helpful for you. You may also want to consider taking a temporary leave of absence rather than withdrawing from your program completely.  

It is your responsibility to inform RMIT before the Census Date of the relevant semester. Census Dates are listed in the Academic Calendar.  

Who is eligible?

All students who are currently enrolled at RMIT Vietnam. 

What you need to know as a student

Students considering cancelling their enrolment are advised to consult with Student Connect Student Advisors prior to making formal decisions.

For international students, if your current visa has been sponsored by RMIT Vietnam, your visa will be therefore cancelled following the Visa cancellation process outlined HERE after the Cancellation of Enrolment request is submitted.

RMIT system/ platforms access loss: Your access to RMIT systems, including email and OneDrive, will be revoked from the effective dates.

Student ID Card: You are required to return your physical student ID card to Student Connect or Campus Security. 

If you cancel your enrolment after the Census Date, you are still liable for tuition and other fees, even if you do not attend classes.

Student Fees and Charges Guide: You can refer to Tuition fees to view deadlines related to fees and charges.

Next steps 

To cancel your enrolment, complete the Program Cancellation form in the Submit Request tile in To cancel your enrolment, complete the Program Cancellation form in the Submit Request tile in myRMIT.

Cancellation of enrolment by RMIT (university-initiated)

RMIT may cancel a student’s enrolment under specific circumstances, including but not limited to: 

Failure to meet enrolment requirements or deadlines 

Non-payment of tuition fees 

Breach of visa conditions or legislative requirements 

Breach of academic integrity 

Submissions of false or incomplete information during admissions 

Being excluded for failing to make satisfactory academic progress 

Being expelled from the university or being deemed a serious concern or risk in relation to the RMIT community. 

These actions are taken in accordance with the RMIT Enrolment Procedure – Discontinuation of Student Program.
